I am a great fan of Window Manager. When I first started out using Linux back in my High School, after getting used to the terminal and other tools. Ubuntu 20.{something} which I was using that time. After that I started exploring cool things I could do with my system. Then I came across this Window Managers.

At first, It looked some strange to me but after trying it out, I felt It was great and giving more powers. And I touch type, this powered me to have the control over all the windows I have spawned up.

With key bindings I could just arrange, resize, kill windows, etc., I could even create my own key bindings to open any apps as well which I regularly use.

For example,
When I press,
- `Mod + Enter` opens my terminal in scratchpad mode
- `Mod + Shift + M` it open youtube-music
- `Mod + E` it opens thunar(File Manager). 

Other thing with the window manager is, it gives you the full control to configure your system to look the way you like.

And I'm still on `X11` and use `bspwm` as my daily driver. I does the job and does it well. And it here it how it looks and I love it.
